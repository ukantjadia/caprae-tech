# 06. People

Researched 2026-09-14 from public sources and LinkedIn. This supersedes the

## The headline correction

You described all five as founders. The research says otherwise, and the
difference is material.

**Kevin Hong is the founder of Caprae Capital.** Press consistently describes him
as founder and Managing Partner. The other four are senior people who run their
own firms or hold operating roles. Three of them carry a "Founder" title on the
Caprae site because they founded *their own companies*, not Caprae.

Zackary Beckham's own LinkedIn headline reads "Partner for Strategic Investments,
ITAD Industry Expert." It does not mention founding Caprae.

Writing "our five founders" on the site would be wrong, and every person in the
ICP knows how to check. The copy fix is in D-009.

## What this changes for the copy

The original pitch was "our developers work with five founders." The accurate and
stronger version:

> Our developers report into a firm run by a Booth MBA who has closed $130M+ in
> deals, advised by a Kellogg MBA who founded an ETA advisory, a Wharton MBA who
> runs an M&A firm, an ASU operator who runs a portfolio company, and a former
> Google Search Ads chief of staff.

Same five people. Every claim checkable. No title anyone can dispute.

Four institutions worth naming: Chicago Booth, Kellogg, Wharton, Google. For a
Pole B audience those carry more weight than the word "founder."
