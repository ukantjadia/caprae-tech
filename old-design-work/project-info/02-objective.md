# 02. Objective

## What we are building

A service-selling website that outsources Caprae's technical team.

Not a portfolio. Not a company brochure. A site whose job is to make a qualified
buyer book a call. [STATED]

## The motive

Caprae has an in-house engineering team that already builds the firm's own
products. That team has spare capacity and a track record. The website turns that
team into a sellable service. [STATED]

## The actual pitch

This is the one sentence the whole site has to earn:

> Our developers already work with founders. Not hypothetically. Daily.

Every generic dev shop says "we understand your business." Caprae can prove it,
because the engineers report into a private equity firm that closes deals with
real operators, and they sit in recurring meetings with those operators. [STATED]

That is the wedge. The site should not lead with stack lists, hourly rates, or
"we do web, mobile, and AI." It should lead with the fact that these developers
have already survived the founder relationship.

## Why a founder-trained developer is worth more

The claim behind the wedge, spelled out so the copy can use it: [STATED]

- They know what a founder actually asks for, which is rarely what they say first.
- They have seen the same requirement change three times in a month and shipped anyway.
- They understand deal context, diligence pressure, and why a deadline is a deadline.
- They do not need a product manager to translate business intent into a ticket.
- They are already in regular touch-point meetings with founders. The communication
  overhead a client normally pays for is already absorbed.

## Primary goal

Qualified inbound. A visitor who owns or runs a company books a call.

## Secondary goals

- Make the technical team legible as a standalone offering, separate from the
  parent firm's deal services.
- Give existing Caprae clients a reason to expand into tech work.
- Stand up as a credibility asset that can be linked from LinkedIn, where Caprae
  already has an audience of 8,000+. [VERIFIED]

## Hard constraint

The parent site already lists Tech Development as a service, priced
project-based. [VERIFIED] This site must not contradict that page on scope,
pricing, or claims. If the two disagree, the parent site wins until the owner
says otherwise.

## What success looks like

No conversion target set, and not a concern for now. [STATED 2026-09-14]

This makes the site a credibility asset first: something to link from LinkedIn,
send to a prospect, and point at during a call. The page still needs one clear
action, but nothing is being optimised against a number yet.

If a target appears later, the structure needs revisiting. A page built to look
credible and a page built to convert are not the same page.
