# 03. Audience

## Demographics

Age 25 to 70. The upper half of that range is not an afterthought. A meaningful
share of buyers are 50+. [STATED]

Design consequence: no 13px body text, no thin grey-on-grey, no interaction that
depends on hover or on noticing a subtle state change. Contrast and type size are
functional requirements here, not accessibility box-ticking.

## Ideal customer profile

Anyone with real authority over a company's direction and budget. [STATED]

- Owners and operators of companies
- Board members and senior executives with decision weight
- Searchers and acquisition entrepreneurs
- Founders
- Investors
- Bankers
- Acquirers, M&A professionals
- People working in financial and commercial banking

The common thread is not industry. It is that the visitor can authorize spend
without asking anyone.

## Overlap with the parent firm

The parent site names its customers as searchers and acquisition teams,
entrepreneurs and founders, PE firms and investment banks, search fund CEOs, M&A
advisors, and independent sponsors. [VERIFIED]

That list and the ICP above are close to identical. The new site is selling a
different product to substantially the same room. Warm audience, not cold.

## What this buyer is actually worried about

Framing for the copy. Not yet validated with real prospects. [OPEN, see Q6]

- Hiring a dev shop that disappears after the invoice clears
- Paying to educate a developer about their business
- Getting a technically correct build that solves the wrong problem
- Losing control of timeline and scope
- Being sold AI as a buzzword with nothing underneath

## What moves them

- Proof they can click on. A live product beats a case study.
- Named people with verifiable backgrounds.
- Evidence of duration. Long relationships, not one-off projects.
- Plain pricing, or at least a plain process for getting to a price.

## What loses them

- Stock photography and stock language
- "Innovative solutions," "cutting-edge," "seamless"
- A form that asks for eleven fields before anyone talks to them
- Any claim without a name or a number attached
