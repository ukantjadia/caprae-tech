Questions

Before you book.

When do you add AI?

Only when it saves real hours or makes the product easier to sell. If it won't pay back, we'll tell you.

Is a human still in the loop?

Where it matters, yes. In Lead QA, AI scores every lead and a human spot-checks the greens and the reds before anything goes out. Every AI step is tested against a human before it ships.

What if something isn't worth building?

We'll say so. Some of our most useful advice is what to leave out.

How do you price automation?

In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.
