Questions

Before you book.

Do I need a technical co-founder to build an MVP?

No. Most of the owners we work with aren't. Tell us the problem in plain words and a founder turns it into the product. A founder takes the CTO seat on your product.

How much does an MVP cost?

In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.

What goes into the first version?

Less than you think. We've shipped too much too early. Now we cut to what a customer will pay for, then build out.

How fast can we launch?

Engineers ship every week, and you see the progress at your weekly check-in. [Content to be added: typical time from first call to launch]

Can I change direction halfway?

Yes. Add, drop or swap features at any point, and the hours move with you.
