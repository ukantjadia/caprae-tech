Kevin Hong is the Founder of Caprae Capital. Before founding the firm, he spent seven years as a business journalist, then scaled two startups to $31M and $7M in annual recurring revenue and raised more than $8M in venture capital.

He holds an MBA from Chicago Booth and is the author of the Amazon bestseller The Outlier Approach. At Caprae Capital he oversees Entrepreneurship as a Service and reviews the firm's Search as a Service work, and he ghostwrites the LinkedIn posts for Branding as a Service.
