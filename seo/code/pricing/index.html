Questions

Before you book.

How do you price?

In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.

Is it a fixed price or hourly?

Hourly, with every feature estimated upfront. You see features × hours × one hourly rate before anything is built, and the hours move only when you change what's in.

What is the hourly rate?

It's fixed per domain and shared on the call, before you commit to anything.

Can I change direction halfway?

Yes. Add, drop or swap features at any point, and the hours move with you.

Who owns the code?

You own what you pay for. The details are in the agreement. confirm with Kevin
