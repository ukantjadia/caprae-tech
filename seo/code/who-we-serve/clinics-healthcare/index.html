Where we help

Where a practice's
hours really go.

It's rarely one big problem. It's a dozen small handoffs nobody's software covers. Here's what we see most, and what we'd build.

Front desk

Intake and registration

What eats the time

Paper forms re-typed into the EHR, insurance cards scanned twice, calls back about what was illegible. Incomplete or wrong registration data is a top-three cause of claim denials, cited by 32% of providers.

What we build

Digital intake and insurance capture before the visit, with a front-desk review queue. Clean data goes into the EHR once.

Scheduling, reminders and no-shows

What eats the time

Phone tag to book, reminders sent by hand, empty slots found that morning. One in three medical groups saw no-shows rise this year, and some now overbook to absorb them.

What we build

Online booking against real provider calendars, automatic reminders, and a waitlist that offers a freed slot to the next patient.

Phones and patient requests

What eats the time

Appointments, refills, results and form requests all come in through the same few phone lines and land on the same few people. Nothing is tracked once the call ends.

What we build

Request forms and routing that turn calls into tracked tasks with an owner, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

Owner and operations

Reporting across locations

What eats the time

Someone exports from the practice management system every Monday and rebuilds the same sheet. By the time it's ready, the week is gone.

What we build

One screen with visits, no-shows, new patients, collections and stuck claims by location and provider, updated daily.

Hiring and onboarding staff

What eats the time

Medical assistants and front-office staff are the roles practices report turning over most. Every exit restarts the hiring and training loop.

What we build

A hiring pipeline that runs the chasing: application, acknowledgement, forms, tracked submission, review and one-click interview booking. We built one for ourselves, and it returns ~20 hrs a week.

You don't fix everything at once. We start with the one that costs your team the most hours, prove it, then move to the next.

Getting paid

We don't replace your billing team.
We hand them cleaner claims.

Your biller or billing partner can only work with what the front desk gives them. When registration data is incomplete or an authorization is missing, the claim is already in trouble. Those are two of the top three causes of denials. We build the front-end tools that get it right the first time, and a shared queue so billing can see what's stuck and why.

What we build
  • Intake and insurance capture with a front-desk review step
  • A prior-auth tracker that billing and the front desk both see
  • A denial log by reason, so the root cause gets fixed instead of reworked

We watch how intake, booking, auths and billing actually run: every paper form, every side spreadsheet, every call. We count the minutes each one adds. That becomes the spec, so we never automate the wrong thing.

Questions

What practices
ask us first.

Do you replace our EHR?

No. Your EHR keeps the chart. We build the intake, scheduling, follow-up and reporting around it, and connect to it where it allows.

Do you replace our biller or billing company?

No. We fix what causes denials before the claim is sent, and give your billing team a clear view of what's stuck and why.

Can you build us an AI scribe?

We'd tell you not to. Good ones already exist. We'll help you choose one, connect it, put data controls around it, and measure the time it actually saves.

Is it HIPAA compliant?

We scope it on the first call, before anything is built. Infrastructure and security run with ITSco, the compliance firm our founders run.

Will it work with our practice management system?

Usually, through its exports or its API. We check yours on the first call. If it can't connect, you know before you pay for an hour.

Can we start with one location?

That's how we prefer it. One site goes live, staff tell us what's wrong, we fix it, then roll it out.
