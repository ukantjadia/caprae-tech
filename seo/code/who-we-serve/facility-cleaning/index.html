Getting found and winning work

Website and SEO

The problem

Facility managers search before they call. An outdated site, or one still showing template text, loses trust before the first conversation, and a company that doesn't rank for "commercial cleaning in [city]" never gets the call at all.

What we build

A fast, real website with a page for every service, industry and city you serve, local SEO so you show up when buyers search, and the proof buyers look for: certifications, the types of sites you clean, response times.

Brand trust before the first call, and inbound leads you don't have to cold call for.

Finding the right prospects

The problem

Cold calling every business in town burns cash fast. Commercial cleaning is a low-moat service, so outreach only works with a tight ideal-customer list and a real reason to switch.

What we build

Prospect lists by facility type, size and region, with the decision-maker's contact. We built Caprae's own sourcing engine: 6 → 50–60 leads an hour.

Your sales time goes to buildings that fit, not to whoever picks up.

Instant quote calculator

The problem

Prospects want a ballpark before they'll book a walkthrough. Without one, many never reach out, and the ones who do may be nowhere near your price range.

What we build

A calculator on your website: facility type, square footage, visits per week and add-ons give an estimated range based on your own past quotes. You still set the real quote after the walkthrough. Every calculator use is a lead with contact details. See the working demo.

More qualified walkthroughs, fewer wasted ones, and a site that looks like a serious operator's.

Running the job

Site SOPs, checklists and proof of service

The problem

Every site has its own protocol, and clients in regulated sites want everything documented. Today that lives in binders, group texts and reports typed after the shift.

What we build

One app, end to end. Each site's SOP becomes a checklist on the cleaner's phone. Photos are logged to the job, time-stamped, geotagged and checked automatically. Updates and issues go in the same place, and audit-ready records build themselves.

Easier to manage, easier to hold the contract, easier to pass an audit.

Crew scheduling and call-out cover

The problem

It's a high-turnover industry. Someone always calls out, and the manager spends the evening on the phone finding cover.

What we build

Shifts by site, one-tap call-outs, and an automatic text to the backups already trained on that site. The first yes takes the shift.

Call-outs stop landing on one person's phone.

Training and onboarding platform

The problem

Every new cleaner gets cross-trained and shadowed before working alone. In a high-turnover business, managers repeat the same training every few weeks.

What we build

Record each training session once. Site-specific guides, videos and SOPs sit in one place for every new hire, with cross-training and shadow-shift sign-offs tracked per person and per site.

Managers spend less time repeating training, and every new hire learns the same standard.

Keeping the client

Supply dashboard for your clients

The problem

Restroom supplies and consumables get refilled on every visit, but the client never sees it, and nobody notices a shortage until a site runs out.

What we build

A dashboard your client can open: what's stocked, what's running low, what was restocked last night, what's on order, by site. Supply levels come straight from the crew's checklist.

A visible reason to stay with you. Hereford's own point: real differentiators win accounts in a low-moat business.

Also possible
  • Client portal and reporting: visits, verified photos and open issues across every site and service, in one view.
  • Issues, root cause and response times: a clock on every issue and a root cause before it closes, matching the 48-hour promise many operators make.
  • Hiring pipeline: application to first shift, including background-check status. We built ours, and it returns ~20 hrs a week.
  • Labor hours and margin by site: time-clock hours against contract hours, so you can see which buildings lose money.

Questions

What cleaning company owners
ask us first.

My cleaners aren't tech people. Will they use it?

If it takes more than a tap and a photo, we built it wrong. One crew tests it before anyone else gets it.

Can't cleaners just upload an old photo?

No. Photos are taken in the app camera, stamped with time and location, and checked against the site and shift. Duplicates get flagged.

Will a quote calculator undercut my pricing?

No. It shows a range based on your own past quotes, never a final price. You set the real number after the walkthrough.

Can the crew app be in Spanish?

Yes. Cleaners pick their language, and supervisors see everything in theirs.

Do you replace our scheduling or time-clock app?

Not if it works. We connect to it and build what it doesn't do.

Do you need to visit our sites?

No. We work from calls and your real documents. On larger projects, an on-site visit can be discussed.
