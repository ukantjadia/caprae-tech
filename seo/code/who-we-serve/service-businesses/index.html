Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be technical?

No. Most of the owners we work with aren't. Tell us the problem in plain words and a founder turns it into the product.

When do you add AI?

Only when it saves real hours or makes the product easier to sell. If it won't pay back, we'll tell you.

How do you price?

In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.

Can I change direction halfway?

Yes. Add, drop or swap features at any point, and the hours move with you.
