Know the company before you call the owner.

Click "research this" on any engagement, and our research agents go well past the company's website. They search the company, its owner, its news and its legal record, compare it to trends in its industry, and pull out the numbers a buyer looks for. Everything lands on one page.

What it's for

  • Deciding whether to follow up: is this owner worth a second call?
  • Preparing for a first meeting: walk in knowing the business, not just its homepage.
  • Staying on top during diligence: new legal cases or news show up in the same portal while the deal is live.

Questions searchers ask us.

Is this different from Caprae Capital's Search as a Service?

Yes. Caprae Capital runs your search for you. We build the tools so your own team can run it, or so you can run it better alongside Capital.

We're self-funded. Is this only for funded searchers or PE?

No. The tools are the same whether you're a traditional or self-funded searcher, an independent sponsor or a small PE team. We scope to the size of your search.

Can it work with our existing CRM or spreadsheets?

Usually. We look at how you search today and build around it. Sometimes that means connecting what you have, sometimes replacing it.

Can you help during diligence?

Yes. The research portal keeps watching the companies in your pipeline for new legal cases and news, and we can run IT due diligence on the target.

Will our deal data stay private?

Yes. Your pipeline and research stay in your own system, visible only to your team.
