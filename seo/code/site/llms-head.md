# Caprae Tech

> Caprae Tech is a founder-led product studio and part of Caprae Capital. Founders who have built, bought and grown companies take the CTO seat on a client's product, decide what's worth building and what will sell, and meet the client once a week, while a small engineering team builds and ships every week. It builds MVPs, business dashboards, deal and financial tools, AI assistants and internal tools for owners in cleaning, healthcare, services and acquisitions who don't have a tech team. Work is priced in hours: features × hours × one fixed hourly rate per domain, agreed upfront.

## How It Works

1. Call: thirty minutes with a founder about what you want to build and what's in the way.
2. Scope: the product becomes a list of features, each with an hour estimate, at your domain's hourly rate. You pick what's in.
3. Build: a founder runs the product like its CTO; engineers ship every week; one check-in a week.
4. Launch & sell: deciding what goes on top, how each feature is presented, and what's worth building next.

Every week: the founder sets priorities on Monday, engineers build and test against how a real user works, the founder reviews whether each feature sells on Wednesday, the client's check-in is on Thursday (demo, decisions, next week), and Friday ships to staging with hours used vs. planned sent to the client.

## Pricing

- Software development is priced in hours, not a lump-sum quote.
- After the first call, the product is broken into features with an hour estimate for each.
- The hourly rate is fixed per domain, shown upfront and shared on the call. Caprae Tech does not publish its rates.
- Features can be added, dropped or swapped at any point; the hours move with them.

## How It Compares to a Dev Shop

- Who decides what gets built: a founder who has shipped products, working with you (a dev shop: you, feature by feature)
- What ships first: what your customers will pay for (a dev shop: whatever is on the list)
- When AI goes in: only where it saves real hours (a dev shop: everywhere, because it sells the project)
- How you're priced: features × hours × one hourly rate, upfront (a dev shop: a lump-sum quote you can't check)
- Your time: one weekly check-in (a dev shop: daily questions and constant reviews)
- After launch: help deciding what to show first and how to sell it (a dev shop: handover and goodbye)

## Frequently Asked Questions

- How do you price? In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront.
- Is it a fixed price or hourly? Hourly, with every feature estimated upfront; the hours move only when you change what's in.

- How much of my time will this take? One weekly check-in.
- When do you add AI? Only when it saves real hours or makes the product easier to sell.
- Is a human still in the loop? Where it matters, yes. Every AI step is tested against a human before it ships.
