## Brand Overview

Caprae Tech is a founder-led product studio and part of Caprae Capital. It builds software for business owners who need it but don't have a tech team, and don't want to become one.

The difference is who runs the product. A founder who has built, bought and grown companies steps in as the client's CTO, decides what's worth building and what will sell, and meets the client once a week. The client runs their business. Engineers build and ship every week.

The studio's view is that anyone can write the code. The expensive part is knowing what to build, what to leave out, and how to sell what ships. AI goes in only where it saves real hours.

Most of what Caprae Tech shows as proof was built for Caprae itself first and runs inside a real business today. See [Work](https://capraetech.io/work/).

## What Caprae Tech Builds

Five things, start to finish. See all at [capraetech.io/what-we-build](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/).

### [MVP development](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/mvp-development/)

From an idea to a working product customers can use, with the features that will sell it at the top. The first version is kept smaller than most people expect: it is cut to what a customer will pay for, then built out.

**Best for:** owners testing a new product line, and non-technical founders.

### [Business dashboards](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/business-dashboards/)

Sales, ops and finance on one screen, pulled from the tools a business already uses: spreadsheets, the CRM and accounting. A founder decides which numbers go on top.

**Best for:** owners and ops leads.

### [Deal & financial tools](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/deal-financial-tools/)

Deal screeners, valuation models and trackers that help evaluate and acquire businesses. The same kind of tooling Caprae uses to find and buy companies.

- Deal screener: scores targets against the buyer's buy box
- Valuation model: stays live and is reused across every target
- Portfolio dashboard: one view across the companies already owned

**Best for:** acquirers and search funds.

### [AI assistants & automation](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/ai-assistants/)

Automations that take repetitive work off a team, added only where they pay back. The work is done by hand first, then what works is automated. Every AI step is tested against a human before it ships.

**Best for:** teams buried in admin.

### [Internal tools & team portals](https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/internal-tools/)

Portals, trackers and workflows a team uses every day, instead of ten spreadsheets. The manual version is mapped first and becomes the spec, so the wrong thing never gets automated.

**Best for:** any team running on spreadsheets.

## How It Works

Four steps. The client hands the product over and Caprae Tech runs it end to end. See [How it works](https://capraetech.io/how-it-works/).

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Call | Thirty minutes with a founder: what you want to build, and what's in the way | You and a founder |
| 2. Scope | The product is turned into features, each with an hour estimate, at your domain's hourly rate. You pick what's in | Founder |
| 3. Build | A founder runs the product like its CTO. Engineers ship every week. One check-in a week | Founder and engineers; you weekly |
| 4. Launch & sell | Deciding what goes on top, how each feature is presented, and what's worth building next | Founder and you |

### A typical week

| Day | Caprae Tech | Client |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Founder sets the week's priorities: what ships and what waits. Engineers build | |
| Tuesday | Engineers build, tested against how a real user works | |
| Wednesday | Founder review: does this feature sell? Features are cut or reordered | |
| Thursday | | Weekly check-in: demo, decisions, next week. Feedback goes into the plan |
| Friday | Ship to staging. Hours used vs. planned, sent to the client | |

## Pricing

Caprae Tech prices software development in hours. There is no lump-sum quote. See [Pricing](https://capraetech.io/pricing/).

1. **We list the features.** After the first call, the product is broken into features with an hour estimate for each.
2. **You see the rate.** A fixed hourly rate for the client's domain, shown upfront.
3. **You choose.** Keep, cut or swap features at any point. The hours follow the choices.

- **Fixed price or hourly?** Hourly, with every feature estimated upfront. The client sees features × hours × one hourly rate before anything is built. The hours move only when what's in the build changes.
- **What is the hourly rate?** It is fixed per domain and shared on the call, before the client commits to anything. Caprae Tech does not publish its rates.
- **Can the scope change?** Yes. Features can be added, dropped or swapped at any point, and the hours move with them.

The pricing page has a sample scope (a cleaning company's first build, 168 hours in scope). It is an example of how a scope reads, not a quote.

## Frequently Asked Questions

See all at [capraetech.io/faq](https://capraetech.io/faq/).

### What is Caprae Tech?

A founder-led product studio, part of Caprae Capital. It builds MVPs, dashboards, deal and financial tools, AI assistants and internal tools for owners without a tech team. A founder takes the CTO seat on the product; engineers build it.

### Do I need to be technical?

No. Most of the owners Caprae Tech works with aren't. Tell us the problem in plain words and a founder turns it into the product.

### What happens on the first call?

You tell us the idea or the problem, no tech words needed. We tell you what's worth building and what isn't, including when AI helps and when it doesn't. Then you get a feature list with hours, at a fixed hourly rate for your domain. You decide from there.

### Is this an alternative to a technical co-founder?

For the product, yes. A founder who has built, bought and grown companies steps in as your CTO, decides what's worth building and what will sell, and meets you once a week. You run your business.

### How do you price?

In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.

### Is it a fixed price or hourly?

Hourly, with every feature estimated upfront. You see features × hours × one hourly rate before anything is built, and the hours move only when you change what's in.

### What is the hourly rate?

It's fixed per domain and shared on the call, before you commit to anything.

### Can I change direction halfway?

Yes. Add, drop or swap features at any point, and the hours move with you.

### How much of my time will this take?

One weekly check-in. The founder in your CTO seat makes the day-to-day calls.

### Who does the work?

A founder takes the CTO/CEO seat on your product and sets the week's priorities. Engineers build and ship every week. You get one check-in a week: demo, decisions, next week.

### What if something isn't worth building?

We'll say so. Some of our most useful advice is what to leave out.

### Can a dashboard pull from the tools we already use?

Yes. Sales, ops and finance come from the tools you already use, such as sheets, your CRM and accounting, onto one screen.

### Can you replace our spreadsheets?

Yes. We map how the work is done today. That manual version becomes the spec, so we never automate the wrong thing.

### When do you add AI?

Only when it saves real hours or makes the product easier to sell. If it won't pay back, we'll tell you.

### Is a human still in the loop?

Where it matters, yes. In Lead QA, AI scores every lead and a human spot-checks the greens and the reds before anything goes out. Every AI step is tested against a human before it ships.

### How do I contact Caprae Tech?

Book a call at [capraetech.io/book-a-call](https://capraetech.io/book-a-call/) or email partners@capraecapital.com.
