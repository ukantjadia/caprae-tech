Tool 1 of 4

Call Intelligence + call QA tracker

60 callersAdmins

Every call our team makes runs through RingCentral. Once a week, Call Intelligence pulls every connected call, scores it, and separates the good calls from the bad ones. The call QA tracker logs every recording, so nothing gets scored twice or missed.

How we use it

  • Scores every connected call and turns the results into KPIs per caller
  • Shows where each caller struggles: handling objections, closing, or something else
  • Gives admins one view of who's performing and who needs help

What changed

We promote callers on their scores, coach the ones who are struggling on the exact part of the call they're getting wrong, and know who's good without listening to every recording. The AI's scores now sit within 0.3 of a human grader's.

Tool 2 of 4

Robo-dialer

CallersManagers

Before our callers pick up the phone, the robo-dialer works through the list and sorts every number: voicemail, receptionist, the owner's voicemail, switchboard or a dead line.

How we use it

  • Tells us upfront which leads are likely to pick up and which aren't
  • Lets us match lists to callers: high-pickup lists go to our best callers, low-pickup lists to the rest of the team
  • Helps decide when to call, and who calls which client and which lead

What changed

Our best callers spend their time on the leads most likely to answer, instead of working through dead lines and voicemails.

Tool 3 of 4

Caprae CRM (with the L2/L3 calling workspace)

13 account managersCallersClients

The CRM replaced the separate lead sheets and engagement sheets every client used to have. Leads, engagements, notes and hours are now in one database that account managers, callers and clients all work from.

How we use it

  • Sends client emails, weekly reports and new engagements automatically
  • Lets clients search and filter leads and engagements by industry, instead of hunting across sheets with random tags
  • Catches duplicate leads, so account managers stop finding them by hand and can top up a list to the right number
  • Shows how many hours went where
  • The L2/L3 workspace gives callers the script, company, contact and call history for every follow-up on one screen

What changed

Account-management overhead dropped by 50%. We put those hours back into what clients actually pay for: more cold calling and more lead generation.

Tool 4 of 4

Recruitment portal

Recruitment team

Our applicant tracking system stopped at the application. Everything after that was manual, so we built the rest.

How we use it

  • Acknowledges every applicant and sends the handbook automatically
  • Tracks each candidate's submission on our own platform
  • Gives the recruitment team an admin portal to verify submissions, with AI analysis alongside
  • Books interviews in one click and emails the booking link

What changed

About 20 hours a week back for the recruitment team, who now review a shortlist instead of chasing every applicant.
