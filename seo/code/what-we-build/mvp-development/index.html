AI MVPs

Many AI products work in a demo and lose money in production.

Every question a user asks costs tokens. Every extra step adds context, and context makes the next call more expensive. If nobody designed for that, the product gets more expensive with every customer it wins. An AI MVP is the cheapest way to find out before you scale.

What we work out in an AI MVP

Cost per use

Tokens per request, across every agent and step, plus the server cost to run it.

Context

How much goes into the main window, what gets handed to sub-agents, and how to keep the context small as conversations grow.

Architecture

Batching and structuring calls so one user action doesn't trigger a pile of separate AI calls.

The business case

What it costs to run against what it earns or saves. Is it worth selling?

What we leave out of v1

Version one is built around what users do every day.

Features people use once a month can wait. Features they need every morning go first. These are the rules we use to decide what makes it into version one, and the real calls we made on our own products.

  1. 1
    Daily beats monthly.Features people need every morning go first. Features they open once a month can wait.
  2. 2
    Don't rebuild what already works.If a tool your users already know does it better, connect to it instead of copying it.
  3. 3
    Every feature has to earn its cost twice.Once in the hours to build it, and again in the server or AI cost to run it.
  4. 4
    Prove the hard part before polishing the easy part.A plain screen on a working engine beats a beautiful screen on a guess.
  5. 5
    Nobody should do the same work twice.If two people can end up on the same task, the product should stop it.

FAQ

Before you book.

Do I need a technical co-founder to build an MVP?

No. A founder from our side takes that seat. Tell us the problem in plain words.

How much does MVP development cost?

It's priced in hours: features × hours × one rate, shown before we start. A proof of concept is a fraction of a full MVP, which is why we often start there.

How long does it take?

4–8 weeks for most MVPs. A proof of concept is usually faster.

What's the difference between a proof of concept and an MVP?

A proof of concept shows it can be built: a working back end with a plain interface. An MVP is something customers can use and pay for.

Can you build an AI MVP?

Yes, and we'll tell you what it costs per use before you scale it.

Can you help us get our first customers?

Yes. Caprae runs outreach, discounted for products we built.

Can I change direction halfway?

Yes. Add, drop or swap features, and the hours move with you.
