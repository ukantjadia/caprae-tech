Data first, not AI first.

Siddhant Pahuja, our Head of AI & Automation, won't open your systems and start building automations. First he looks at the data. A dashboard serves many use cases at once, so getting the data right matters more here than anywhere else.

  1. 1

    A week on your data

    What you have, where it lives, which KPIs you need and which automations you want.

  2. 2

    Outputs first

    We agree on the numbers and decisions the dashboard has to produce before we touch the inputs.

  3. 3

    The data map

    How data flows in, where it's stored and how the database is structured to support every output.

  4. 4

    Formulas you sign off

    For every KPI and every automation, we show the formula and the data behind it. You approve it before we build.

    When the data isn't there, we say so

    Some features need data you don't collect yet. We'll tell you to start recording it now so it's ready later, or we'll drop the feature. Putting AI on a problem without the right data or documentation just creates a mess faster.

  5. 5

    Build

    Only what the data can support.

Why not just use Power BI or a spreadsheet?

If all you need is charts from one clean source, you should. We build when the dashboard has to run work, send reports or show clients their numbers, not just display them.

Can it pull from the systems we already use?

Usually. The data review tells us where your data lives and how to bring it together. If something can't connect, you know before you pay for it.

How often does the data refresh?

It depends on the use case: live where a decision needs it, daily or weekly where it doesn't.

What if our data is messy or incomplete?

That's what the data review is for. We'll tell you what can be built now, what needs data you should start recording, and what to drop.

Can our clients see it?

Yes. A client-facing view shows each client only their own numbers.

How long does it take?

It depends on the data and the automations. You get hours per feature after the data review.
