The case

Problem. Build. Result.

01 · Problem

Companies are replacing hardware faster to keep up with AI, and every retired server or drive still holds data. One that leaves the building unwiped is a breach waiting to happen.

02 · What Destroy Drive built

A global on-site service: data wiping to NIST, DoD and NAID standards, physical destruction, disintegration and degaussing, de-racking, secure packing and logistics, and full data center decommissioning.

03 · Result

Projects for a publicly traded asset manager, a global top-10 bank and a Fortune 500 telecom, including 100,000+ drives wiped across three data centers.
