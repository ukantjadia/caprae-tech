The story

A storytelling studio needed a site that tells its own story.

Simba Studio helps founders, operators and category builders turn what they do into stories people remember, mostly on LinkedIn and in their own voice.

A studio that sells storytelling has to tell its own story well. The website had to explain how the studio works, show what it makes and make the price clear, without sounding like every other agency.

We built the site on its own domain and set it up for search from day one: page titles and descriptions, structured data and a sitemap in place at launch.
