The story

Most founder content says nothing. Simba writes the other kind.

Feeds are full of fluff: no point in ten words, no numbers, no structure, written for likes rather than trust. Simba's view is that good writing is a P&L statement: every word has a job, and every paragraph needs a number.

The studio interviews the founder, studies the market, builds a story arc and trains custom LLM models to write in the founder's own voice. What comes out is LinkedIn content, case studies and research reports built for dwell time, authority and pipeline.
