The story

Every call heard. Every caller coached.

A sales manager can listen to a handful of calls. Not every call, every day. So most coaching came down to gut feel and the few calls someone had time to hear.

Call intelligence listens to all of them. Each recording is matched to its call and its lead, the short and empty ones are set aside, and every real conversation is transcribed and scored by AI on how the caller opened, how they sounded and how clearly they pitched.

The results add up per caller: who is on track, who is developing, and who needs help with the pitch first. It also shows the hours when owners actually pick up, client by client. What started as Caprae’s own tool is now a product: Call Intelligence™, live in beta from Cold Call Killers.
