Problem. Build. Result.

Most small and mid-size companies can't afford a full IT, security and compliance team, but they still face the same audits, ransomware and downtime as the big ones.

02 · What ITSco built

One partner that runs all of it: managed IT and 24/7 helpdesk, managed security operations, compliance as a service (HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI DSS, NIST, CMMC), cloud, and fractional CIO, CTO and CISO leadership.

03 · Result

Thirty years of repeat clients across healthcare, finance, legal and manufacturing, and published results like a 14-location rehab network taken to SOC 2 Type 2 to win a state contract.
