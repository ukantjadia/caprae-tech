The story

Clients stopped waiting for Friday.

Caprae runs outreach for around thirty client businesses at a time. Every week, each client wants the same answers: which owners were called, who was interested, and what happens next.

Those answers used to live in spreadsheets. Leads were copied from one sheet to another by hand, and the week’s calling results went out as screenshots in a Friday email.

Caprae CRM puts all of it in one place. Each client logs in to their own account and sees their leads, the week’s calls and every interested owner, with the full call history behind each one. They can message their account manager, and they’re told the moment something new comes in.
