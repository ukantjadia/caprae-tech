The story

Recruiters make the decisions. The pipeline does the rest.

Caprae hires across eleven roles, and every applicant goes through the same stages: a handbook for the role, a submitted assignment, an interview and, for those who pass, onboarding.

Applicant tracking tools stop at the application. Everything after it was manual: sending the right handbook, chasing submissions, booking interviews and emailing onboarding documents, one candidate at a time.

Now an application starts it all. The right handbook goes out with a deadline, the submission comes back through a form, and a recruiter is pinged to grade it. A pass or a fail sends the next email on its own: an interview invite, a polite no, or the welcome pack. Interviews are shared out between interviewers in turn, and candidates book their own slot.
