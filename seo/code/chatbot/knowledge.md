## 1. Company facts
Page: https://capraetech.io/ and https://capraetech.io/why-founder-led/

- Name: Caprae Tech. Part of Caprae Capital (capraecapital.com), the parent company.

- Tagline: "Dev shops build what you ask. Founders build what sells."

- Who it is for: owners in cleaning, healthcare, services and acquisitions who need software but don't have a tech team.
- Contact: email partners@capraecapital.com, or book a call on the Book a call page (https://capraetech.io/book-a-call/). The booking form asks for full name, email, an optional LinkedIn URL and how we can help; we email you to set the time. The first call is thirty minutes with a founder.
- Proof: every tool shown on the site runs inside a real business, either Caprae's own, a client's, or a company the founders built. "We built it for ourselves first."

## 3. MVP development
Page: https://capraetech.io/what-we-build/mvp-development/

- MVP development for non-technical founders: from an idea to a working product your customers can use, with the features that will sell it at the top.
- Best for: owners testing a new product line, and non-technical founders.

## 9. How it works
Page: https://capraetech.io/how-it-works/

Four steps. You hand it over; we run it end to end.
1. Call: thirty minutes with a founder. What you want to build, and what's in the way. (You and a founder.)
2. Scope: we turn it into features with an hour estimate for each, at your domain's hourly rate. You pick what's in. (Founder.)
3. Build: a founder runs your product like its CTO. Engineers ship every week. You get one check-in a week. (Founder and engineers; you weekly.)
4. Launch and sell: we decide what goes on top, how each feature is presented, and what's worth building next. (Founder and you.)

A typical week:
- Monday: the founder sets the week's priorities (what ships and what waits). Engineers build.
- Tuesday: engineers build, tested against how a real user works.
- Wednesday: founder review: does this feature sell? Features are cut or reordered.
- Thursday: your weekly check-in: demo, decisions, next week. Your feedback goes into the plan.
- Friday: ship to staging. Hours used vs. planned, sent to you.

Who does the work: founders decide, engineers ship. One chain of accountability, from the founder in your CTO seat to the person writing the code.

## 10. Pricing
Page: https://capraetech.io/pricing/

- Software development, priced in hours. No random quote and no lump-sum quote.

## 11. How Caprae Tech compares with a typical dev shop
Page: https://capraetech.io/why-founder-led/

- Who decides what gets built: a dev shop: you, feature by feature. Caprae Tech: a founder who has shipped products, working with you.
- What ships first: a dev shop: whatever is on the list. Caprae Tech: what your customers will pay for.
- When AI goes in: a dev shop: everywhere, because it sells the project. Caprae Tech: only where it saves real hours.
- How you're priced: a dev shop: a lump-sum quote you can't check. Caprae Tech: features × hours × one hourly rate, upfront.
- Your time: a dev shop: daily questions and constant reviews. Caprae Tech: one weekly check-in.
- After launch: a dev shop: handover and goodbye. Caprae Tech: help deciding what to show first and how to sell it.

Lessons the founders work by:
1. Features don't sell themselves. The order you show them in does.
2. The first version should be smaller than you think.
3. AI where it pays back. Nowhere else.
4. Do it by hand before you automate it.
5. A founder's time is the most expensive thing in the building. Yours included. That's why you get one weekly check-in, not daily questions.

## 15. Frequently asked questions
Page: https://capraetech.io/faq/

- Do I need to be technical? No. Most of the owners we work with aren't. Tell us the problem in plain words and a founder turns it into the product.
- What happens on the first call? You tell us the idea or the problem, no tech words needed. We tell you what's worth building and what isn't, including when AI helps and when it doesn't. Then you get a feature list with hours, at a fixed hourly rate for your domain. You decide from there.
- Is this an alternative to a technical co-founder? For the product, yes. A founder who has built, bought and grown companies steps in as your CTO, decides what's worth building and what will sell, and meets you once a week. You run your business.
- How do you price? In hours. We list the features, estimate the hours for each, and show your domain's hourly rate upfront. You choose what's in.
- Can I change direction halfway? Yes. Add, drop or swap features at any point, and the hours move with you.
- How much of my time will this take? One weekly check-in. The founder in your CTO seat makes the day-to-day calls.
- Who does the work? A founder takes the CTO/CEO seat on your product and sets the week's priorities. Engineers build and ship every week. You get one check-in a week: demo, decisions, next week.
- What if something isn't worth building? We'll say so. Some of our most useful advice is what to leave out.
- Can a dashboard pull from the tools we already use? Yes. Sales, ops and finance come from the tools you already use, such as sheets, your CRM and accounting, onto one screen.
- Can you replace our spreadsheets? Yes. We map how the work is done today. That manual version becomes the spec, so we never automate the wrong thing.
- When do you add AI? Only when it saves real hours or makes the product easier to sell. If it won't pay back, we'll tell you.
- Is a human still in the loop? Where it matters, yes. In Lead QA, AI scores every lead and a human spot-checks the greens and the reds before anything goes out. Every AI step is tested against a human before it ships.
- Code ownership, contracts, data security and compliance: covered on the first call and in the agreement. The site does not publish details, so don't state any.
