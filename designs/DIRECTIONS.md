### Raises taken from declined challengers

Discipline, not clothes. Each written into the assigned direction as its own line.

- **From Fletcher:** the idea arrives before the text does. Every specimen plate
  must communicate what the product is before its caption is read. If a plate
  needs its caption to make sense, the plate is wrong.
- **From Jacquard:** full traceability from artifact back to the individual
  decision. Every claim on the page carries its source inline, not in a footer.
  A number without a provenance mark does not ship.
- **From Iridescent cloud:** colour confined to hairline edges while the text
  field stays achromatic. The specimen sheet gets exactly one hue and it lives
  on rules and marks, never on a fill.

## Direction A — The Specimen Sheet (assigned, index 7)

**THESIS.** Four products exist and strangers pay for them. This page treats each
one as a specimen on a plate: examined, measured, labelled, provenance attached.
It refuses the category default of a card grid of logos with adjectives, because
a card says "we made a thing" and a specimen says "here it is, at this scale,
measured, and here is where the number came from".

**OWN-WORLD.** Plate ground in warm archival paper. A single hairline system:
0.5px rules that box each specimen, tick marks along the top edge of every plate
at a fixed interval so the plates read as measured rather than framed. One hue,
a deep iron oxide, used only on rules, tick marks, and provenance marks. Never a
fill, never a button background. Type is a text serif with a true small-caps for
labels, paired with a tabular-figure grotesque for every measurement. Provenance
marks are a superscript numeral plus a rule leading to the source, set inline.

**STORY.** The visitor understands that this team ships real software, not
proposals. They believe it because every claim carries a source they can follow
without leaving the sentence. They act by clicking a specimen through to a live
product, then booking.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Left two thirds: the headline set in the text serif at large
size, ranged left, three lines maximum, leading the page with nothing above it,
and the primary action directly beneath, no more than 480px from the top edge.
Right third: the first specimen plate, SaaSquatch Leads, shown at true scale with
its tick-marked top edge and two measurements visible. The plate is cropped by
the viewport edge deliberately, so the sheet continues past the fold. Proof strip
of the remaining three specimen names sits on the fold line.

## Direction B — The Tearsheet (Impeccable's pick)

Offered as a single pick card, not a ranked alternative. It leads nothing.

**THESIS.** The buyer reads deal tearsheets all day. This page is one: the
engineering team presented in the exact document format the visitor already
knows how to scan. It refuses the marketing-page arrangement entirely.

**OWN-WORLD.** Near-white stock, a hard 12-column rule grid that is visible, not
implied. Everything tabular. Figures in tabular lining numerals, right-aligned,
always. Section headers as ruled bands with a reference number. Two weights of
rule only: hairline for row separation, 2px for section boundary. One accent,
used on the single most important figure per band and nowhere else.

**STORY.** The visitor recognises the format in under a second, which buys
trust before a word is read. They scan for the figures they always scan for.
They act because the CTA sits where the contact block sits on a real tearsheet.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Masthead band with the sub-brand and a document reference.
Beneath it a two-column summary: left, the positioning statement in running
text; right, a key-figures table with six rows. Primary action in the top-right
of the masthead, where a tearsheet puts the banker's contact.

**HONEST RISK.** Familiarity. This is the most legible direction and the least
surprising one. A buyer may register it as competent and not remember it. It is
my top-ranked candidate precisely because it is safe, which is why the roll did
not assign it.

## Direction C — The Event Display (fused challenger, competitive)

Held the product-clarity axis against the assigned direction. Kept as a full
alternate rather than absorbed.

**THESIS.** Origination is the product. This page holds one frozen deal event
and lets the visitor move outward through it, the way a detector display holds
one collision. It refuses the scroll-narrative arrangement: nothing advances
because you scrolled, it advances because you selected.

**OWN-WORLD.** Near-black vacuum ground. Concentric ring section as the
compositional law, so the page composes in rings rather than bands. Detector
rings in muted steel. Tracks in two signal colours by type: sourced and closed.
Energy bars in a third, used only for figures. Spare technical grotesk, labels
pinned to tracks with leader lines, momentum figures in tabular digits.

**STORY.** The visitor sees a mechanism rather than a claim. They believe it
because they can isolate one track and read its numbers. They act from a
persistent action that never leaves the frame.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** The ring section occupies the centre right at fixed aspect,
one event held. Headline and primary action pinned top-left over the vacuum
ground, never overlapping the rings. Track labels resolve in after the rings
settle.

**HONEST RISK.** This is the only direction that puts a dark ground in front of
a Pole B buyer, and the research says light mode signals institutional
credibility for this audience. It is also the only one that will not exist for a
visitor with reduced motion unless the static tier is genuinely designed, not
screenshotted.

## The standing door

The category standard, played straight: a light marketing page with a hero, a
logo strip, three feature cards, a testimonial, and a footer CTA. Executed at
full fidelity it would work. It is here because it is always the user's option,
not because it is recommended. Taking it means naming two or three products this
should sit alongside so their craft level becomes the bar.

## Amendment, 2026-09-14

The craft floor bans a kicker or eyebrow above a heading outright: "no brief
earns it back". Direction A's FIRST VIEWPORT originally opened with a small-caps
eyebrow. Removed. The headline leads with nothing above it.

Two further floor rules bind all three builds and were not in the contracts:
the accent may never appear as a fill, so primary actions are ink-filled and the
hue stays on rules and marks; and same-size cards of heading plus text are
refused as page structure, so specimens are full-width plates, not a grid.

## Direction D — The Core (assigned, re-roll 1, index 4)

**THESIS.** Diligence is drilling. You extract a core and read what the strata
say, in order, with depth attached. This page is that core: scroll is descent,
depth is time, and each stratum is something this team shipped. It refuses the
scroll-reveal arrangement, where motion is a curtain that opens on arrival.
Here motion is the instrument, and the visitor turns it.

**OWN-WORLD.** Bone page stock under instrument light, with one graphite core
column running the full height as the only dark mass. A single calibrated rail
sits off-centre; every stratum, product and figure registers against it as a
1px mark. State is line form, never hue: full-height solid is live, dashed is
pending, half-height is archived, a doubled line outranks its neighbours. One
hue, sodium, and it exists only against the graphite core where it has contrast.
On the bone page there is no colour at all. Type is a technical grotesk for
display, a humanist sans for reading, and a mono reserved for depth readouts
and measurements, which is measurement, not costume.

**STORY.** The visitor descends and the record accumulates: four products, real
dates, real figures, each arriving at its own depth. They believe it because the
depth readout and the rail never stop agreeing with the text. They act from an
action that is on screen at every depth.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Headline ranged left across the upper two thirds of the bone
page, nothing above it. Primary action directly beneath, within 480px of the top
edge. The graphite core enters at the right, cropped by the viewport edge, with
the depth readout at 0.0m pinned beside it on the rail. The first stratum sits
just below the fold line so the core visibly continues downward.

**FORM.** The Core. Position 4 of 7 on my re-rolled grounded list. Seed key
fb60c0c1, re-roll 1, assigned index 4.
