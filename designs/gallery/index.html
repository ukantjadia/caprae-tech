CAPRAE ENGINEERING / DESIGN OPTIONS

How to use. Both options run live, side by side, at the same viewport width so the comparison is fair. Click a panel to load it, click the header Open to go fullscreen with a switcher bar.

Viewport resizes both at once. Check that the CTA stays above the fold at 400 and at 1440; that is a build gate, not a preference.
Reduced motion reloads both with ?motion=off. Option 4 drops to its static poster and never creates a WebGL context. That tier has to be a good page on its own.

Only one WebGL context runs at a time. Panels stay shaded until you click them, and Option 4 pauses its loop when scrolled out of view. Four live contexts would choke the browser, which is exactly the constraint the production build has to respect too.
