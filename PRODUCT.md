# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with React islands. Confirmed by the owner 2026-09-14. React is used only
where an island genuinely needs it (3D, interactive components); everything else
ships as static HTML and CSS. Package manager is `bun`. Deploy target undecided.

## Users

Primary: people with authority over a company's direction and budget, aged 25 to
70, with a meaningful share over 50. Owners and operators, board members, senior
executives, searchers and acquisition entrepreneurs, founders, investors,
bankers, acquirers, and M&A professionals.

The common thread is not industry. It is that the visitor can authorize spend
without asking anyone.

Situation: evaluating whether to hire an outside engineering team, usually on a
work laptop, often after a referral or a LinkedIn post. Job to be done: decide
whether these people can build the thing, and whether they will understand the
business context without being taught it.

This audience overlaps almost exactly with the parent firm's stated customers:
searchers and acquisition teams, entrepreneurs and founders, PE firms and
investment banks, search fund CEOs, M&A advisors, independent sponsors. Warm
audience, not cold.

## Product Purpose

Sell Caprae Capital's in-house engineering team as an outside engagement.

The team already builds the firm's own software. This surface turns that team
into a sellable service and produces qualified inbound: a visitor who owns or
runs a company books a call.

No conversion target has been set. The owner has confirmed this is a credibility
asset first, something to link from LinkedIn and send to a prospect. If a target
appears later the structure needs revisiting.

## Positioning

The engineers report into a private equity firm that closes deals with operators,
and sit in recurring meetings with them. They have already survived the founder
relationship.

A neighboring dev shop cannot truthfully copy this. Every agency claims business
understanding; this one is structurally inside the deal business.

## Operating Context

Four service lines, all confirmed by the owner 2026-09-14:

1. Dedicated engineers, monthly
2. Fixed-scope projects, MVP through full platform
3. Fractional CTO and technical advisory
4. AI-readiness and automation work

Constraint: capraecapital.com already lists Tech Development as a project-based
service, alongside Search as a Service, Entrepreneurship as a Service,
AI-Readiness as a Service, Post-Acquisition Strategy and Branding as a Service,
three of which are published at $1,250/mo. This surface must not contradict that
page on scope or price. AI-readiness in particular overlaps an existing service
line and needs a boundary, or the two pages compete.

Evaluation ritual: this buyer checks things. They will open LinkedIn and verify a
title. They will click a product link and use it. Anything unverifiable is worse
than absent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Team size publicly reported as 20+ engineers. Current headcount and how much is
available for outside work are undecided.

Engineering spans full-stack application development, ML and AI workflows, data
pipelines and scrapers, telephony and outbound systems, and open-source platform
work.

Undecided and not to be invented: pricing for this surface, the conversion
mechanism (booking link, form, or email), the deploy target and domain, the
length of the developer relationships in years, the meeting cadence with
founders, and whether any client can be named.

Terminology that matters to this audience: searcher, ETA, independent sponsor,
origination, diligence, SDE, close date, LOI.

## Brand Commitments

Sub-brand under Caprae Capital, confirmed by the owner 2026-09-14. Named in the
current builds as "Caprae Tech". Inherits the firm's credibility and may
use firm-wide figures provided they are labeled as firm-wide, not as engineering
output.

No brand kit, fonts, or logo files have been supplied. Colour and typography are
delegated.

Accessibility is a conversion requirement, not a compliance exercise, because of
the 25 to 70 audience. Body copy 17px minimum, no hover-only interactions,
`prefers-reduced-motion` honoured as a first-class tier. The owner's own machine
runs with reduced motion enabled, so that tier is not hypothetical.
